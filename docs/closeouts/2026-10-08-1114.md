## Decisions Made

- **Refuse, don't disclose, a file with no email header** — owner ruling. The
  header set is deliberately broad: refusing a real sparse message is worse than
  converting it with gaps disclosed.
- **Attachment pages are reproduced to their CropBox, as a viewer shows them**, with
  the clipping disclosed. Alternative (reproduce the full MediaBox, revealing what
  the original hides) was flagged to the owner as their call; not overruled.
- **Test material lives outside the served tree** (`dev/`) rather than being
  deleted by the build, so a plain build never dirties the git tree.
- **`EML_TOOL_VERSION` stays `1.0.0`** — this is the first shipped version.
- **Re-review scoped to the session's range only**; the other 40 commits were
  reviewed in September and untouched.
